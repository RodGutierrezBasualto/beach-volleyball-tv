# Updating the Beach Volleyball TV website

A quick guide for whoever looks after the website. No technical knowledge needed.

## Logging in

1. Go to **beachvolleyballtv.com.au/admin**.
2. Enter the username and password you were given.
3. You'll land on the **Dashboard**, which shows what's on the site and has quick buttons for common jobs.

Whatever you save goes live on the website after **about a minute**, while the site rebuilds itself. A brand-new page may show "not found" until then, so give it a minute and refresh.

## Add an event

1. Click **Events** in the left menu, then **+ Add new event**.
2. Fill in the boxes. Each one says what it's for:
   - **Event name** and a **short summary** (shown on the event cards)
   - **Full description**. Leave an empty line between paragraphs.
   - **When**: the date and start time (the finish time is optional). For weekly games, fill in **Repeats** (e.g. "Every Tuesday"). If there's no fixed time, use **Time note** instead, e.g. "10am till late" or "Check Instagram to see if it's on".
   - **Where**, **Price**, **Who it's for**, and **What's included** (one item per line)
   - **Sign-up button**: choose **Open a web link** and paste a WhatsApp or Instagram group link, or point it at the contact form.
3. On the right, pick the **Event type** and add an **Event photo** (see "Photos" below).
4. Click **Publish**.

Tick **Feature this event** to show it large at the top of the Events page.

## Write a news story

1. Click **News**, then **+ Write new story**.
2. Add a **Headline** and a short **Summary**.
3. Write the story in the big text box, just like a Word document. Use the toolbar above it:
   - **Normal text / Heading / Subheading**: pick a style for the line your cursor is on.
   - **B**, *I* and U: bold, italic and underline. Select the words first, or switch on and type.
   - **Bullet list**, **numbered list** and **quote** buttons.
   - **Link**: select some words, press **Link** and paste the web address. To change or remove a link, click inside it and press **Link** again.
   - **Photo**: adds a photo where your cursor is. You can also drag a photo straight into the text, or paste one.
   - **Video**: paste a YouTube link. The title fills in by itself.
   - **Undo / Redo**: the arrows on the right.
4. Already written it in Word or Google Docs? Copy and paste it in. Headings, bold, lists and links come across, and messy formatting is cleaned up. Add photos afterwards with the **Photo** button.
5. On the right, choose a **Category**, add a **Main photo** and click **Publish**.

To change a photo inside a story, use the buttons under it: **Caption & description**, **Replace photo** or **Remove**.

## Photos

Use any photo, from your phone or camera, at any size.

1. Click **Choose photo**, or drag a photo onto the box.
2. A cropping window opens. **Drag** to move the photo and use the **Zoom** slider (or your mouse wheel) to zoom in.
3. Keep the important part, such as faces and the ball, **inside the dotted line**. That area shows everywhere on the site.
4. Click **Use this photo**. It's resized and compressed for you, and you'll see how much smaller it got (e.g. 4.2 MB → 190 KB).
5. Fill in **Describe the photo** in a few words. This helps people who use screen readers, and helps Google.

iPhone photos in HEIC format can't be opened on some computers. If you see an error, export or share the photo as a JPG first.

## Drafts

Not ready to publish? Choose **Draft** under **Publish** and save.
- Only you can see a draft (use **Preview draft** to check it; you need to be logged in).
- Switch it to **Published** when it's ready.

## Delete old events and stories

- **One item:** hover over it in the list and click **Delete**, or open it and click **Delete** in the Publish box.
- **All finished events at once:** go to **Events**, click **Select all past events**, then **Delete selected**.

You'll always be asked to confirm. Deleting can't be undone.

Finished events disappear from the website's "upcoming" lists automatically soon after midnight (Sydney time) the day after they end, so deleting them is just tidying up.

## Videos

1. Click **Videos**.
2. Paste a YouTube link and click **Find video**.
3. Check the title, pick a **Category**, optionally add a short description, and click **Add to library**.

In the library you can edit titles, move videos up or down, tick **Featured** for the video to show first, or remove one with **✕**. Removing a video here doesn't delete it from YouTube. Click **Save changes** when you're done.

To show a video on the **Athletes** page as a profile, type the athlete's name in its **Athlete name** box and save.

## Handy tips

- **Ctrl + S** (or **Cmd + S** on a Mac) saves the page you're editing.
- If you try to leave a page with unsaved changes, you'll get a warning.
- If something can't be saved, a red box at the top explains what's missing.
- Click **View website** in the left menu at any time to see the live site.
- When you're finished, click **Log out**.
